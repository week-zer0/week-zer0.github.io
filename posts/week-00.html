Starting at zero

Hi, nice to meet you and welcome to this random web page. So, to preface: I have loved music all my life. I am the type of person who gets super hyper-fixated on one song and then listens to it over and over until it gives me the ick. Then I stop listening to it altogether, only to rediscover it 6–9 months later and repeat the cycle. You know… healthy. Nothing remotely neuro-spicy here.

Anyways. I always wanted to make music, but I am also hella lazy. Or maybe lazy isn’t quite the right word. I want to do it, and sometimes I even start doing it, but then I abandon it because I am not good at it. I hate being bad at things. So much.

I have spent most of my adult life being afraid to try things because I am not good at them! But I want this to end. I want to teach myself that it is okay that I suck, and so this page is dedicated to that.

I am basically starting at zero. Week Zero (;

I don’t know much about music production, but I have done some research because I want to build myself a nice home studio to start with. I will say that, admittedly, the stuff I picked is maybe a little bit spicy for a starting-out setup. I know that. I may or may not be bidding on an OP-1 on eBay right now.

But there is a reason to this madness. I am very much into aesthetics, and I know that if I find something beautiful, I will have an easier time using it. So I thought for Week Zero, while I wait for all my equipment to come in, I would make an aesthetic pin board of the gear I am sourcing and the vibe I am going for in my studio.
